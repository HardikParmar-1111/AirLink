Cryptographic Privacy

Security Designed into Every Packet

Unlike proprietary tools that tunnel your sensitive screen and clipboard data through foreign cloud servers, AirLink stays strictly inside your room.

100% Local Network

Packets never leave your local Wi-Fi router. There is zero external cloud relay, zero SaaS subscriptions, and zero tracking.

AES-256-GCM Encryption

All video chunks, clipboard snippets, and touch coordinates are encrypted using military-grade AES-256-GCM with authenticated tags.

Replay Attack Protection

Every WebSocket message utilizes a strictly randomized, unique 12-byte Initialization Vector (IV) to prevent packet replay or spoofing.

Zero Analytics or Telemetry

No Google Analytics, no Firebase Crashlytics, no third-party SDKs. Your screen content and keystrokes belong only to you.
